🍽️ Grub \& Giggles – South Indian Restaurant Website



A modern, responsive restaurant website for Grub \& Giggles, showcasing authentic South Indian cuisine through an attractive and user-friendly interface.



✨ Features



Responsive Design – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile.



Interactive Food Menu – Browse dishes with category filters, prices, discounts, and ratings.



Food Gallery – Visual showcase of South Indian dishes.



Special Offers – Limited-time deals with a countdown timer.



About \& Restaurant Story – Restaurant introduction and journey timeline.



Chef Profiles – Meet the restaurant's culinary team.



Customer Reviews – Testimonials and feedback.



Table Reservations – Form for booking tables.



Blog Section – Articles on South Indian cuisine and recipes.



Contact \& Newsletter – Contact form and email subscription interface.



🛠️ Tech Stack



* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript
* Bootstrap
* Font Awesome



🍛 Menu Categories



Dosa | Idli | Ghee Pongal | Mini Tiffin | Poori | Rava Upma
